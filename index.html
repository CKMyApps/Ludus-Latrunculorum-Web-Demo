How to Play

You command the ivory pieces. The pyramid is your Dux.

  • Move any piece horizontally or vertically through empty squares.
  • Capture a soldier by moving to trap it between two of your pieces. At a corner, occupy both neighboring squares.
  • Win by blocking every path out for the opposing Dux. The edges and friendly pieces can also block it.
  • If a position repeats three times or the move limit is reached, the side with more soldiers wins. Equal counts give a draw.
